How the model was calibrated

  • Built from ~65 real WBGT readings (Aug 10 – Sept 4): under 86°F almost always Level 0–1 regardless of sun; 86–89°F holds Level 1 even in full clear sky; 90–95°F is the real transition zone; 96–102°F typically Level 3; 103°F+ typically Level 4.
  • Dry heat still counts — a real heat wave hit Level 3–4 with humidity as low as 28–35%.
  • Sustained multi-day heat holds WBGT elevated for hours across a whole practice window, unlike scattered-storm days which spike and drop fast.

How to read this: this site cross-references real weather with your trainer's actual WBGT readings so you can see how sun, humidity, sustained heat, and time of day move the needle for your program specifically. It does not measure WBGT itself and cannot see direct sun exposure, wind, or radiant heat off your field the way the handheld device does. Per MSHSAA policy, a scientifically-approved WBGT instrument must be used on-site whenever temps exceed 80°F, with readings taken hourly starting 30 minutes before practice. Use this site to plan ahead — not to replace that reading. Forecast data refreshes automatically every Saturday morning; it is not live minute-to-minute.
